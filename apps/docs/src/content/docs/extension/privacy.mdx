_Last updated October 22, 2026._

**penciled for the browser**, the penciled.org highlighter extension, is a private, local-only extension: it doesn't collect, send or sell any data, and it makes no network requests. In short: **nothing leaves your browser.**

## Does penciled send my highlights anywhere?

No. penciled makes no network requests. It has no servers, analytics, tracking, ads or accounts, so there's nowhere for your highlights to go.

## What does it store, and where?

Only what it needs to show your highlights again, in your browser's extension storage:

- **Your highlights and notes**, with the address and title of each page you highlight on. They're kept on your computer only, and are deleted when you uninstall penciled.
- **Your settings**: the toolbar theme, whether selecting text opens the toolbar, and your colors. If you're signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser syncs them to your other computers, as it does for any extension's settings.
- **The sites you turned penciled off on**, on your computer only.

## Does it read the pages I visit?

Only to find your highlights on them. It doesn't read or keep anything from the pages you visit, except the text you highlight and a little of the text around it, which it needs to find the highlight again.

## Why does it ask for these permissions?

- **Read and change data on all websites**: so you can highlight on any site. penciled reads a page's text to find your highlights on it, and adds its toolbar on top. The page's own content isn't changed: highlights are drawn by the browser.
- **Storage** and **unlimited storage**: to keep your highlights and settings, without a size limit.

## How do I export or delete my highlights?

From the notes page, at any time: export a backup or a Markdown file, or delete highlights. Turning penciled off for a site stops it from running there at all.

## How can I check these claims?

penciled's [source code](https://github.com/marcellosso/penciled/tree/main/apps/extension) is public, so anyone can read exactly what it does.

## What if this statement changes?

The new version will be published here, with its date. Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/marcellosso/penciled/issues).
