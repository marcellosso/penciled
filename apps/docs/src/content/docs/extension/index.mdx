**penciled for the browser** is the penciled.org browser extension: a free, open-source highlighter for Chrome, Edge and Firefox that saves text highlights and notes on any website, locally and with no account. Select text to highlight it or add a note, and your highlights come back when you return to the page.

![An article with yellow and blue highlights, and penciled's toolbar open over a selected sentence.](../../../assets/extension.png)

It's free and [open source](https://github.com/marcellosso/penciled/tree/main/apps/extension), with no account to create. Your highlights are saved in your browser and never sent anywhere: see the [privacy statement](/extension/privacy/).

## How do I install it?

Get penciled for your browser:

- [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/edpobhdkceoadekhpdagmnknocckaaje)
- [Firefox Add-ons](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/penciled-highlighter/)
- [Edge Add-ons](https://microsoftedge.microsoft.com/addons/detail/penciled-%E2%80%93-highlighter-/abhbgdlbgleahgeodcifnpecikbdciml)

It works in Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers, version 123 or later, and in Firefox 140 or later. Other Chromium browsers, like Brave, install it from the Chrome Web Store, and Firefox-based browsers, like Zen, from Firefox Add-ons.

## How do I use it?

- **Highlight:** select text and pick a color in the toolbar. Click a highlight to change its color, add a note or remove it.
- **The sidebar:** click penciled's toolbar button to keep your highlights open beside the page as you read. **This page** follows the tab you're on, and **All pages** searches everything. Click a highlight to scroll to it, or the eye to hide or show highlights on the tab. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> opens or closes the sidebar too.
- **The menu:** if you'd rather have a small menu of this page's highlights, make penciled's toolbar button open it in the settings. The menu also turns penciled off for the whole site. In browsers without a sidebar, the button always opens the menu.
- **Tags:** in the sidebar, click a highlight's tag button to add tags, such as `#to-read` or `#research`. Click a tag to show only highlights with it, or search for `#tag` there or on the notes page.
- **All your notes:** the notes page lists every highlight from every site, with search. Open it from **All notes** in the sidebar or the menu.
- **Keyboard:** <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>A</kbd> moves into the toolbar, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>]</kbd> and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>[</kbd> move between highlights, <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>H</kbd> hides or shows highlights on a tab, and <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> opens or closes the sidebar. On a Mac, <kbd>Alt</kbd> is <kbd>⌥</kbd>.
- **Settings:** choose the toolbar's theme, what penciled's toolbar button opens, turn off opening the toolbar on every selection, edit or add colors, and see the sites penciled is off on.

## How do I back up my highlights?

Highlights are stored only in the browser you made them in, and uninstalling penciled deletes them. To keep a copy, or to move them to another browser, use **Export a backup** on the notes page, then **Import a backup** in the other browser. **Export as Markdown** gives you a readable copy for your notes app.

## What can't it do yet?

- Highlight text inside frames, such as embedded posts, or in PDFs.
- Highlight inside text fields and other editable areas, which it leaves alone.
- Sync highlights between computers. Your settings do sync, through your browser account.

When a page changes so much that penciled can't be sure where a highlight belongs, it doesn't guess: the highlight is listed as **Couldn't find this on the page**, so you can still read or delete it. See [Anchoring](/guides/anchoring/) for how this works.
